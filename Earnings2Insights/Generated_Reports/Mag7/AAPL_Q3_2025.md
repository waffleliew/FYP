## 1. Financial Analysis

### Key Highlights

- **Revenue & Growth**: Revenue grew by 5.08% YoY to $95.36 billion, showing consistent performance in global markets. Expectation-beating EPS of $1.65 with a 7.84% YoY rise indicates robust business fundamentals.
- **Financial Efficiency**: Gross Margin rose slightly to 47.05%, reflecting improved production efficiencies. However, the Operating Margin dropped QoQ highlighting seasonality impacts.
- **Investment in Growth**: Significant YoY increase in Capex (+53.80%), focused on AI and infrastructure aligning with growth strategies.
- **Balance Sheet Strength**: Maintained a strong cash position though Cash & Cash Equivalents saw a QoQ decline, focusing on strategic investments.
