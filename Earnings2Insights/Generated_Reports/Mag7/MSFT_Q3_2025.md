- **Revenue Growth**: Revenue grew by +13.28% YoY and +0.62% QoQ, driven by strong cloud and AI demand.

- **Operating Cash Flow**: Recorded a +66.21% QoQ increase, underscoring enhanced cash flow generation and cost management.
