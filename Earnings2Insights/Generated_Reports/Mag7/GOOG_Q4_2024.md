## 1. Financial Analysis

### Key Highlights
- Alphabet achieved consolidated revenue of $96.5 billion in Q4 2024, reflecting a 12% increase in both reported and constant currency terms.

- **Recommendation**:
  - **Next Day**: Buy, given the strong financial and operational performance metrics.
  - **Next Week**: Hold, monitor market and regulatory developments closely, especially potential impact from AI advancements.
  - **Next Month**: Buy, focus on long-term growth prospects with continued AI and Cloud advancements bolstering revenue streams.

- **Catalysts**:
  - Launch of new AI-driven products and services that could enhance engagement and monetization.
  - Continued expansion of Google Cloud and strategic partnerships with major industrial players.
