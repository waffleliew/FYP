## 1. Financial Analysis

### Key Highlights
- Revenue grew +10.5% YoY and +18.2% QoQ, reflecting a strong holiday season with robust AWS performance.
- Gross Margin decreased QoQ to 47.34% due to increased logistics costs but improved over last year indicating efficiency gains.
- Operating Margin increased to 11.29% YoY and QoQ, showcasing effective cost management and profit growth.
- The company’s net debt position improved significantly, underscoring enhanced liquidity and reduced leverage.
- Capex saw a 90.4% YoY increase, signaling continued investment in AWS and logistics capacity enhancements.
