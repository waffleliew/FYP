# Amazon.com (AMZN) Investment Report - Fiscal 2024 Q3

---

## 1. Financial Analysis

### Key Highlights
- Amazon reported third-quarter revenue of $158.9 billion, marking an 11% increase year-over-year, excluding foreign exchange impacts.
- Operating income reached $17.4 billion, a significant 56% increase from the previous year.
- Trailing 12-month free cash flow adjusted for equipment finance leases was $46.1 billion, up a staggering 128% or $25.9 billion year-over-year.
- AWS saw a 19.1% year-over-year growth, reaching a $110 billion annualized revenue run rate.

*These highlights provide a snapshot of Amazon's financial performance, showcasing robust growth across key financial metrics.*

### Concluding Summary

Amazon's financial performance in Q3 2024 indicates strong growth and strategic advancement. The company's significant revenue and net income growth, coupled with effective cost management and reinvestment in key growth areas such as AI and cloud infrastructure, highlight a robust financial stance. The year-over-year improvement in operating and net margins demonstrates enhanced operational efficiencies. However, substantial investments reflected in the Capex/OCF ratio indicate a focus on long-term capabilities, particularly in AI and infrastructure development, which hold potential for future growth. The negative net debt position underscores a robust liquidity and financial health. Amazon is well-positioned to leverage these investments into sustained growth, making it a strong contender in the evolving tech landscape.
